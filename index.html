why it failed

"Fastest" and "high-performing" are hype superlatives. Translated literally, they read as unverified marketing claims to a DE engineering lead.

the anchor

nachvollziehbar (traceable, verifiable) is a term DE technical buyers search for directly — it does the trust-building work "fast" was trying to do.

the restructure

Leads with function, not speed — matching how DE product documentation is conventionally ordered.
